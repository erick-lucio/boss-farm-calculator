How to get your POESESSID

  1. Click "Open pathofexile.com/trade" below and log in if needed.
  2. Press F12 to open your browser's DevTools.
  3. Go to the Application tab (Chrome/Edge) or Storage tab (Firefox).
  4. In the left sidebar: Cookies → https://www.pathofexile.com.
  5. Find the row named POESESSID and copy its Value.
  6. Paste it into the POESESSID field on this page.

This value logs you in as you — treat it like a password. It only ever goes from your browser to this site's own backend, never anywhere else.
